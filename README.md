# Local SEO Playbook

How to rank a service business in the Google Map Pack. The exact sequence I follow for every local SEO engagement, in priority order: Google Business Profile, NAP/citations, review strategy, on-page local SEO, local link building, then measure and maintain.

GBP fixes show movement in 2 to 4 weeks, citation work in 1 to 2 months, and competitive Map Packs typically take 3 to 6 months of consistent work.
