# Local SEO Playbook

How to rank a service business in the Google Map Pack. This is the exact sequence I follow for every local SEO engagement, in priority order.

## The short version

Map Pack rankings come down to three things: a strong Google Business Profile, consistent citations, and reviews. Everything below serves one of those three.

---

## Phase 1: Google Business Profile (weeks 1 to 2)

The GBP is the single highest-leverage asset in local SEO. Fix it first.

1. **Claim and verify** the profile. One profile per real location.
2. **Categories** — pick the single most accurate primary category, then add every relevant secondary category.
3. **Business name** — the real business name only. Keyword stuffing risks suspension.
4. **Description** — use the full 750 characters. Say what you do, where, and for whom.
5. **Hours** — accurate daily hours plus holiday hours. Wrong hours lose trust and rankings.
6. **Services and products** — list everything with one-line descriptions.
7. **Attributes** — select every applicable attribute.
8. **Photos** — logo, cover, exterior, interior, team. Then add fresh photos monthly. Businesses with 100+ photos get measurably more calls.
9. **Posts** — publish weekly: offers, updates, events. UTM-tag the links.
10. **Q&A** — seed common questions with owner answers, then monitor for misleading public answers.
11. **Reviews** — see Phase 3.

Run the [gmb-audit-checklist](https://github.com/JahanzaibShahzad/gmb-audit-checklist) to score the profile before and after.

## Phase 2: NAP and citations (weeks 2 to 4)

Google trusts businesses whose name, address, and phone number are identical everywhere.

1. **Decide the canonical NAP** — one exact spelling of the name, one address format, one phone number. Write it down.
2. **Fix the website first** — NAP in the footer and on the contact page, matching the canonical version exactly. Add LocalBusiness schema markup.
3. **Core citations** — build or correct listings on the top 30 to 50 directories: Google, Bing, Apple Maps, Yelp, Facebook, Yellow Pages, and industry-specific directories (Avvo for lawyers, Healthgrades for clinics, Angi/HomeAdvisor for contractors).
4. **Data aggregators** — submit to the major aggregators so downstream directories pick up the correct data.
5. **Audit quarterly** — citations drift. Recheck the top 20 every quarter.

## Phase 3: Review strategy (ongoing, starts week 1)

Reviews are a confirmed Map Pack ranking factor and the first thing customers read.

1. **Ask every happy customer** — the best time is right after the service is delivered.
2. **Make it frictionless** — a short review link or QR code. Every extra click halves the response rate.
3. **Respond to every review** — positive and negative, within 48 hours. Thank positive reviewers by name. Answer negative reviews professionally, never defensively, and take the resolution offline.
4. **Never buy reviews** — fake reviews get filtered and can trigger penalties.
5. **Track velocity** — a steady few reviews per month beats 50 at once followed by silence.

## Phase 4: On-page local SEO (weeks 3 to 6)

1. **One location page per location** — unique content, embedded map, NAP, local testimonials, driving directions.
2. **Service + city pages** — one page per (service x city) combination that has real search demand. Each page needs genuinely unique content, not find-and-replace duplicates.
3. **Title tags** — primary keyword plus city, under 60 characters.
4. **Internal linking** — location pages link to service pages and back.
5. **Run the on-page checks** — use the [seo-audit-toolkit](https://github.com/JahanzaibShahzad/seo-audit-toolkit) on every new page.

## Phase 5: Local link building (month 2 onward)

Local links beat generic links for Map Pack rankings.

1. **Local sponsorships** — youth sports teams, community events, local charities. Most link back from their sponsors page.
2. **Local press** — new location openings, awards, community work. Local journalists need stories.
3. **Partnerships** — complementary local businesses link to each other (a plumber and an electrician serving the same town).
4. **Industry directories** — the niche directories competitors are listed on.
5. **Scholarships** — a small annual scholarship for a local school earns .edu links, but only do this if it is genuine.

## Phase 6: Measure and maintain

- **Rank tracking** — track Map Pack positions with a grid tracker (positions vary by searcher location, a single city-wide rank is misleading).
- **GBP insights** — calls, direction requests, and website clicks from the profile, month over month.
- **Review count and rating** — vs the top 3 competitors.
- **Quarterly mini-audit** — re-run the GBP checklist and the citation audit every quarter.

## Timelines

Honest expectations I set with every client: GBP fixes show movement in 2 to 4 weeks, citation work in 1 to 2 months, and competitive Map Packs typically take 3 to 6 months of consistent work. Anyone promising page one in a week is selling something else.
